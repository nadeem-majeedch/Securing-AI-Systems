## Before you submit a lab

- [ ] `python labs/student/<id>/starter.py` runs to the end without an exception.
- [ ] My output matches section 9 of the manual, or I can explain every difference.
- [ ] Every student task has a written answer that refers to a number I produced.
- [ ] At least one challenge exercise is attempted, even if incomplete.
- [ ] I can state the defensive control the lab demonstrates without looking at my notes.
- [ ] I did not open `labs/solutions/<id>/` until after my first attempt.
